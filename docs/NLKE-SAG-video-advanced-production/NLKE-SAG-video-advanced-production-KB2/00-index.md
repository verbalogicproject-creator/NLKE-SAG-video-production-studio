# NLKE-SAG Video Advanced Production — Knowledge Base #2 (OpusClip-Class Gaps)

This KB fills the 12 gaps identified between a basic AI video assembly pipeline and a production-grade, OpusClip-class SaaS product. It covers both the **ML/algorithmic differentiators** that make clips actually perform, and the **enterprise engineering backbone** required to run this reliably, safely, and profitably at scale.

1. `01-semantic-clip-discovery-scoring.md` — Finding the right moments and predicting their performance (virality/hook/retention scoring)

3. `03-intelligent-reframing-crop-trajectories.md` — Smart crop, temporal smoothing, multi-aspect-ratio output
4. `04-animated-captions.md` — Word-level karaoke/kinetic captions
5. `05-broll-retrieval-insertion.md` — Semantic b-roll matching, evidence-safety, insertion workflows
