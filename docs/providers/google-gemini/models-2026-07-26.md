## Production selection policy

- **Gemini Omni Flash** (`gemini-omni-flash-preview`) is the default for multimodal,
  conversational video generation and editing. It accepts text, image, audio,
  and video context and supports multi-turn refinement through Interactions.
- **Veo 3.1** (`veo-3.1-generate-preview`) is selected for native audio,
  scene extension, first/last-frame control, and reference-image workflows.
- **Veo 3.1 Lite** (`veo-3.1-lite-generate-preview`) is restricted to previews
  and inexpensive iteration.
- **Lyria 3 Clip/Pro** provide soundtrack preview and production music.
- **Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS** provides controlled narration and dialogue.
